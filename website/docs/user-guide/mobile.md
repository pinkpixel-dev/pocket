# Mobile & Progressive Web App (PWA)

Pocket is engineered mobile-first. You can install Pocket as a Progressive Web App directly to your smartphone or tablet home screen for a fast, standalone application experience.

## Installing on iOS (iPhone & iPad)

1. Open **Safari** and navigate to your Pocket instance (e.g., `https://pocket.yourdomain.com`).
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Tap the **Share** button in the Safari toolbar (the square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Confirm the name and tap **Add** in the upper-right corner.

Pocket will appear on your home screen with its custom icon. When opened, it runs in standalone mode without Safari browser chrome, navigation bars, or URL inputs.

## Installing on Android

1. Open **Chrome** or **Firefox** and navigate to your Pocket instance.
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Tap the three-dot menu icon in the top right corner.
4. Select **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
5. Follow the on-screen prompt to confirm installation.

## Mobile interface features

* **Bottom navigation bar:** Access your library, collections, search, and settings comfortably with one thumb.
* **Touch-friendly tap targets:** Every button, card menu, and tag filter is spaced with a minimum 44px tap target.
* **Fast search:** Tap the search icon to immediately pull up the keyboard and filter your library.
* **Card actions sheet:** Tapping the menu on any bookmark card opens a native-feeling bottom action sheet to edit, copy, pin, or delete links.
* **Adaptive card grid:** On small screens, Pocket automatically scales preview cards into a single or dual-column stream optimized for one-handed scrolling.
